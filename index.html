Welcome to TiPToe

Your sports news digest, built for busy students who want to stay informed without spending hours reading.

  • 📰Live sports headlines — fetches today's top stories across all major sports directly from NewsData.io.
  • 🏷️Category filters — quickly narrow stories by sport (Football, Basketball, Soccer, etc.) using the chips at the top of the news panel.
  • 🔍Keyword search — search by team name, athlete, or topic to find specific stories.
  • ✨AI summaries — click any article to instantly get a concise summary of the key points in the right panel. Powered by Claude (optional — works with article descriptions if no Claude key is set).

To get started, add your NewsData.io API key via the ⚙ API Keys button in the top right.

API Keys

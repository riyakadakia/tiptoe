Welcome to News Flash

Your news digest — built for busy students who want to stay informed on current events, fast.

  • 📰Live headlines — fetches today's top news stories directly from NewsData.io.
  • 🏷️Topic filters — narrow down by sports, politics, tech, and more using the category chips.
  • 🎨Color-coded topics — each story card is color-coded by topic for quick visual scanning.
  • ✨AI summaries — click any article for a concise breakdown. Powered by Claude via OpenRouter if you add a key; uses article descriptions otherwise.
  • 📖Reading tracker — sign in with a username to track which articles you've read and review your history.

Add your NewsData.io key via ⚙ Keys in the top right to get started.

API Keys

Keys are stored only in your browser's localStorage and sent only to the respective APIs.

NewsData.io (Required)
Fetches live sports headlines. Free tier at newsdata.io.
OpenRouter (Optional — AI Summaries)
Enables AI-generated article summaries via Claude on OpenRouter. Without this, article descriptions are used. Get a key at openrouter.ai.
News Preferences
Shapes what articles appear in your feed. Changes apply on next reload. Sign in to save preferences per account.

Sign In

Enter a username to track the articles you read. No password required — this is stored locally on your device.
